You've found my node on the web, where I share **what I build, what I learn and what I'm curious about.**

I believe a website should feel like the person behind it. What follows is my story, compressed to a few paragraphs.

It all started with machines I wasn't supposed to open. No internet, no manuals, **just a screwdriver and a lot of trial and error.**

Back then machines told you what was wrong by sound. A tone pattern, a clicking drive, a fan spinning up for no reason, a power supply with a new whine.

Those machines taught me debugging, and with it logical thinking. Break it, observe, fix it, repeat.

Somewhere along the way the passion became a career. Support first, obviously.

I never stopped. Only the machines changed: **software, then systems, then platforms.**

SaaS taught me what scale means. One fault, thousands of users, no place to hide. I've had my hands on the infrastructure underneath, the databases and servers behind it, and the features on top.

Lately I've been drifting toward **solution architecture**, designing how the pieces fit before anyone builds them.

Right now I build **AI-accelerated workflows and products** for enterprises. Same curiosity, new machines.

On the side, I'm obsessed with **agents**: how to keep them reliable, and how to apply the latest research.

Always up for **a good conversation** and connecting with new people.
